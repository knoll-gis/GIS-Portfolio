About Me

I'm a GIS Technician II and E-911 Address Coordinator for Guadalupe County, TX, focusing on address management, street naming, land records, and public safety datasets. My work centers on automating repetitive tasks and building reliable GIS workflows that reduce errors and speed up operations.

I design ArcGIS Pro Tasks, Python toolboxes, and Excel-integrated systems that streamline everyday workflows such as address verification, parcel selection, and street-name conflict checks.

I graduated from Texas State University in 2024 with a B.S. in Geographic Information Science and a minor in Nature and Heritage Tourism, which grounded me in both the technical side of GIS and how people experience and interact with place.

ArcPy · Metaphone · Fuzzy Matching

Street Name Conflict & Phonetic Matching Tool

A street-name conflict detector combining substring matching, Metaphone pronunciation encoding, and fuzzy string scoring to catch sound-alike names across centerlines and reserved-name datasets.

Impact: Reduced confusing street names, improved 911 routing clarity, and increased review consistency.
